## 2026-09-29: EA fix after a missed 10:00 entry
- **Reported:** no trade at 10:00 server on the MetaQuotes demo (XAUUSD H1 chart).
- **Cause (likely):** v1.00 marked the day as attempted before reading D1
  history; on an H1 chart D1 data may not be loaded yet -> silent skip all day.
- **Fix (v1.01):** preload D1 in OnInit, retry through the 10:00-10:59 window
  until data is ready, lock the day only after an order is accepted (up to 5
  send retries), log every skip reason, show next entry / waiting reason on chart.
